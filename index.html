BEFORE YOUR FIRST MOVE

A few good
questions.

What can I customise?+

Choose your board finish and size, then mix a piece style with your favourite colour. Add up to 24 characters of engraving to the front edge for a final personal touch.

Can I save a design and come back?+

Yes. Save your set and you’ll find it in the bag icon at the top of the page. Designs are stored in this browser on your device. You can also download a text summary to keep or share.

Can I see different angles?+

Use “Rotate view” in the customiser to see your board from the other side. The live illustration updates as you choose colours, piece styles and engraving.

How do I order my set?+

This is a concept storefront, so ordering and payments aren’t connected yet. The collection, imagery and pricing are illustrative. You can explore the customiser and save a design without making a purchase.
